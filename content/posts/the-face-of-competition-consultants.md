This is what you get when you blend 2,131 competition consultants into one face.

{{< average-consultant >}}

I used every public headshot from the competition teams of 18 consultancies in the US and Europe. That includes economists, data scientists, analysts and partners: anyone on a competition, merger, litigation or arbitration team, but not academic affiliates.

The picture is made in five steps, and nothing is edited by hand.

**Collect.** A script visits each firm's people pages and keeps the people on its competition teams, 2,277 in total.

**Clean.** A face detector puts 478 points on each face. Photos with no face, more than one face, a face too small to read, a head turned more than 20 degrees, or the firm's grey placeholder are dropped, which leaves 2,131 faces.

**Line up.** Each photo is rotated, scaled and shifted so the eyes and mouth land in the same spot.

**Stretch.** Each face is gently warped to the average face shape, so noses sit on noses and chins on chins. Without this step the result looks like a ghost.

**Average.** Every pixel of the final picture is the average colour of that pixel across all 2,131 photos.

The photos are only used to make the blend. They stay private and are never shown, and no single face can be picked out of 2,131. I'll run the same recipe again in a few years, so the next face can sit next to this one.

The idea comes from a post showing the average face of a YC founder.
