Charts make it so much easier to make a case.

Every page of every published EU merger decision since 1991, one pixel each. 3,168 coloured pages out of 131,359. Almost none before 2000. Spreading since 2012.

{{< now-in-colour >}}

Each pixel is one page. Pages run in date order, top to bottom, column by column.

Grey means the page is black and white. Colour means it isn't.

A coloured page can hold many colours. The pixel shows only one: the colour that covers most of the page. Very pale tints are skipped, so the faint edges around a chart's lines don't win over its bars.

Colour usually means a chart. Sometimes it's a map, an organigram, a company logo or a highlighted box. Blue hyperlinks and the EU flag in the letterhead are left out.

Hover a pixel for its decision and page. Click to open it.

Don't read the count as exact.
